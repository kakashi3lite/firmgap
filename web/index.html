Model notes

  • Investor NPV = PV of incremental cash vs. interim-only: gross margin before energy on extra compute − extra energy − penalties − genset − deferral fees − SLA penalties − BESS CapEx (committed at interim energization) + max(post-firm arbitrage over BESS life, resale). Discounted monthly at WACC.
  • Queue carry is excluded from NPV. It contains delayed revenue, which the margin term already counts, and tied-up capital is sunk in both pathways. Carry stays in the case's all-in $/MWh KPI only.
  • Downside NPV assumes the bridge is built but fails its witness test: no bridge months, and the BESS is resold or kept for post-firm arbitrage.
  • Engineering derates: the control deadband lowers the effective ceiling, the usable SoC window scales energy, and availability scales BESS power. CapEx stays on nameplate.
  • Compliance = certified (every event covered in its first minutes) ∧ shortfall < 0.5 MWh/yr ∧ SLA-late energy < target.
  • Monte Carlo re-draws the congestion year (event count, timing, duration); load and tariff stay fixed.
  • Not modelled yet: historical nodal LMP replay, ramp-rate limits on shed and rebound, ride-through requirements, battery degradation, demand charges and capacity value, intra-year seasonality in monthly phasing.
