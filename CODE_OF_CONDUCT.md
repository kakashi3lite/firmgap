# Code of conduct

firmgap is a technical project about energy, money and reliability, so disagreements about
assumptions are expected and welcome. Argue with evidence, not with people.

**Expected:** be respectful and specific, cite sources for modelling claims, assume good faith, and
credit others' work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised language, or
sustained disruption.

**Enforcement:** report incidents privately to the maintainer (see `SECURITY.md` for the private
channel). The maintainer may edit or remove content and restrict or ban participants. Decisions are
final for this repository.
